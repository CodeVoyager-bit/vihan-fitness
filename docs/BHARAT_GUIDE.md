# Vihan Fitness app — guide for Bharat sir

Open the app on your phone, log in with your number, and tap **Share → Add to Home Screen** so it opens like any other app.
The bottom bar has five buttons: **Today · Members · ＋ Record · Inbox · More**. Language: tap EN / मरा / हिं at the top.

> The app never collects money. People pay you in cash or UPI as always; the app only keeps the record.

## Every day (1 minute)

1. **Today** shows money received this month, what's outstanding, what's overdue (after the 10th), and anything waiting for you.
2. Tap the red-dot numbers to deal with **claims**, **registrations** or **help requests**.

## Someone pays you

1. Tap the big **＋ Record** button → pick the member (people who owe are on top).
2. The amount is filled in — change it if needed. Choose **Cash / UPI / Other**, date is today.
3. Look at **This covers**: the app pays the oldest unpaid month first. Extra money becomes advance for coming months. To put it on a different month, tap **Change split**.
4. Tap **Review** → **Save payment**. Only when you see **Payment saved ✓** is it recorded.
5. Optional: **Send receipt on WhatsApp**.

Made a mistake? Open the member → **Payments** → **Reverse** and write why. Nothing is deleted; the history shows what happened.

## A member says "I have paid"

Their claim appears in **Inbox → Payment claims**. Their balance does **not** change until you act:
- **Confirm & record** — you received it; this records the payment.
- **Link to this payment** — you already recorded it yourself; this marks the claim done without counting it twice.
- **Reject** — write the reason (e.g. "UPI didn't arrive"); the member sees it.

## Reminders on WhatsApp

- **More → Follow-ups → Fees** lists overdue members. Tap the green button, check the message (English/मराठी/हिन्दी), then **Open WhatsApp** and press Send yourself.
- If the member has a claim waiting, the app warns you first so you don't chase money that's already paid.
- The app only notes that WhatsApp was *opened*; it can't know if you sent it.
- **Follow-ups → Attendance** lists active members with no *recorded* visit for 8+ days (people sometimes forget to scan). Paused, left and opted-out members never appear, and anyone you messaged in the last 7 days is hidden.

## New members

1. Print the **registration QR** (More → QR codes) or share the link. People register with name, number and password.
2. **Inbox → Registrations** → **Approve**. Choose the month their billing starts. Only if you've *checked* an older balance (e.g. from the notebook), enter it under **Verified older dues**.
3. If a name matches an existing member, the app warns you — it may be the same person with a new number.

## Pause, leave, come back

Open the member → **Profile** → **Change status** → Active / Paused / Left and the month it starts. The app shows which unpaid months will be cancelled; earlier dues stay. Coming back? Set **Active** — cancelled months from that month on are restored.

## Discounts, extra or old dues

Member → **Fees**: **Waive** part or all of a month (with a reason), or **Add charge** for a confirmed older balance. Waivers can be undone.

## Forgot password

Check it's really them, then member → **Profile** → **Create password reset code**. Tell them the 8-letter code in person or on a call. It works once, for 30 minutes, on the **Forgot password?** page.

## Check-in QR at the door

Print **More → QR codes → Check-in QR**. Members scan it, log in once, and tap **Check in** — one visit per day. It's an honour system for goals and encouragement, never for money. If a poster is copied around, tap **Make a new check-in QR** and reprint.

## Monthly challenge

**More → Monthly challenge** → pick *Reach your goal*, *Back on Track* or *Bring Your Gym Buddy* (the buddy offer runs only with the offer you write and approve). Members choose to join and whether their first name is shown when they finish. No rankings.

## Keep your records

**More → Export records** — download Members, Charges, Payments, Claims and Attendance as Excel-friendly files, or a full backup. Do it at the end of every month and keep a copy.
